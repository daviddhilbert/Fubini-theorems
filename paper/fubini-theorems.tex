\begin{abstract}
Let two definable maps in an algebraically closed valued field of
equicharacteristic zero have the same target. If their fibres admit
parameter-definable RV lifts with equal bounded residue-field values, then
their total spaces have equal bounded motivic values over the original
parameter base. We give a proof using finite monomial decompositions,
first-order certificates for Euler characteristics, and stabilized
bijections witnessing equality in the residue-field quotient. The same
method gives a geometric Fubini theorem in the affine-localized target.
Over the complex Laurent-series field, finite Kummer coordinates identify
the residue-field quotient with the equivariant Grothendieck ring and give
a vanishing criterion for morphisms of complex varieties.
Both Fubini theorems hold for fibre representatives of mixed degree.
For a definable family indexed by the value group, we compute the bounded
value of its inflated total space by a finite partition of the parameter
space, and deduce the corresponding formula for the motivic volume over
the Laurent-series field.
\end{abstract}
